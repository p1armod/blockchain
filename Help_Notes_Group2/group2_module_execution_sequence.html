Day 1 to Day 3

Write Smart Contract Functions

Member 2 (API)
Build Express Controllers & Routes
• POST /api/land/joint
• POST /api/land/:id/consent

• Co-Owner Consent Panel

Day 4

Secret Trick: Bina Wait Kiye Parallel Kaam Kaise Karein

Member 2 aur Member 3 ko Day 1 par kya karna chahiye jab tak Member 1 ka chaincode ready na ho:
